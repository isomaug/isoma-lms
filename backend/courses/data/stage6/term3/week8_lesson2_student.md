# Week 8 - Lesson 2

## Session length
**60 minutes**

## Finish the week
* Write a short reflection: what worked, what broke, what you would add next term
* Peer-review one other project using three stars and one wish

## Continue
1. Complete the reflection sheet (5–8 sentences).
2. Visit one other team's station; give three stars and one wish on their card.
3. Receive feedback on your own project and note one change you agree with.
4. Pack kits carefully; return sensors and leads to the correct bags.

## Check your understanding
* Hand in reflection + peer card to you before you leave.

## Key words
* reflection
* peer review
* three stars and a wish
