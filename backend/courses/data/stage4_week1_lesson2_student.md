# Week 1 - Lesson 2

## Session length
**About 30 minutes** (finish Week 1)

## Today you will finish learning
- How to explain what each block does in your own words
- How to improve Button A so it shows your initial
- How to check inputs and outputs on your Micro:bit
- How to pack away safely and tick what you completed

## Finish the week

### 1. Step 3 — Explain your blocks (about 7 minutes)
Look at your programme again.

Before you download any change, say out loud what each block does:
- What does **on start** do?
- What does **on button A pressed** do?
- What does **on button B pressed** do?

If you are working with a partner, swap roles: one person explains, the other downloads.

### 2. Step 4 — Extension (about 5 minutes)
Can you change Button A so it shows your **initial** instead of a smiley face?

Try it. Download. Test.

Extra challenge (if you finish early): programme Buttons A and B pressed together to show a star icon.

### 3. Share what you built
Demonstrate your Micro:bit.

Hold the device and explain one block using the correct words:
- input
- output
- algorithm
- programme

### 4. Check inputs and outputs
Answer these for yourself:
1. Point to an **INPUT** on your Micro:bit.
2. Point to an **OUTPUT** on your Micro:bit.
3. What is the **algorithm** for your Button A programme?

### 5. How hard was it?
For each skill, choose: easy / medium / hard
- Connecting the USB
- Opening MakeCode
- Downloading the programme

### 6. Coming next week
Next week you will **read** someone else's code and predict what it does before you run it — just like reading a book.

### 7. Wrap-up — unplug safely
1. Unplug the USB cable carefully.
2. Put the Micro:bit kit back in the kit box.
3. Close MakeCode tabs.

### 8. Tick what you can do
On your worksheet, tick:
- [ ] Name scrolled on the LED
- [ ] Button A works
- [ ] Button B works

## Check your understanding
Say one sentence that uses the word **input**.

Say one sentence that uses the word **output**.

Example ideas:
- Pressing Button A is an input.
- The LED face is an output.

## Key words to remember
- algorithm
- input
- output
- variable
- debug
- LED
- programme
- MakeCode
- download
- simulator

## Stay safe online and on the device
Do not programme surnames, phone numbers, or home addresses as scrolling text. Use a first name or a made-up name only.
