## Stay safe
Safeguarding Note: All project ideas must be reviewed and approved by the you before building begins. Projects that monitor or track people (motion detectors aimed at individuals, sound recorders, proximity alerts aimed at other you) must be declined and redirected to a general engineering problem. Circuit builds with multiple sensors require you verification of all wiring before USB connection — two sensors simultaneously increase the risk of incorrect connections. All personal data in design briefs (first name only) must be confirmed compliant before submission.
