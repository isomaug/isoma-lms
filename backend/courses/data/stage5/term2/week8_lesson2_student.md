# Week 8 - Lesson 2

## Session length
**About half of this week's plan** (finish what you started in Lesson 1)

## Finish the week
- Deliver a 5-minute formal presentation to a panel of 2 following the full required structure: Problem -> Solution -> Live Demo -> Evidence -> Reflection (including explicit comparison to Term One project)
- Demonstrate a working Term Two prototype live: the project must respond to input and produce correct output without you assistance, and must incorporate at least one named Term Two concept
- Present at least one quantified piece of evidence from testing — a statistic, measurement, or calculated value — displayed in a graph or table during the presentation
- Answer at least 2 panel questions with reasoned, technically precise responses that demonstrate understanding of the code, the data, and the design decisions
- Complete a 7-question peer feedback rubric for a classmate including written comments on Q6 (Term Two concept) and Q7 (code quality)

## Continue

### SCORING, MODERATION AND CERTIFICATES  (30 min)
1. You presents in order. You times with a visible timer. Partner sits adjacent to operate the prototype during the Demo section if needed — the presenter must narrate what the partner is doing: "My partner is now pressing button 1 to trigger the GREEN state."
2. Panel Q&A (3 minutes for you): you ask at least 1 question; assessor asks at least 1 question. Term Two-specific panel questions the assessor is briefed to choose from: "Explain why you chose that specific threshold value in your classifier." "What would happen if the temperature sensor read an extreme value of 50°C — would your FSM handle it correctly?" "You used an array — what is stored at index 0 and at the last index?" "How does your project relate to the AI concepts you studied in Week 6?" "If you were to add a third sensor, which one and why?" "Your Reflection mentions Term One — what is the single most important technical skill you learned between Term One and Term Two?"
3. Audience (other you): complete the 7-question peer feedback rubric during each presentation. Q6 and Q7 require careful attention — Q6 asks you to identify and describe the Term Two concept; Q7 asks them to evaluate code quality from the information given in the presentation.
4. Panel scoring: you and assessor complete the score sheet independently for each presenter immediately after their Q&A. Do not wait for all presentations to finish. Moderate after all presentations are complete if scores differ by more than 10%.
5. After all 8 presentations: you and assessor confer. Moderate any score with a discrepancy greater than 10% using the design brief, sketch, and test results log as evidence. Finalise all panel scores before the certificate calculation phase.
6. Calculate you's Term Two weighted total score using the full DDD Part 9 formula: Participation 10% (8-week average on 3-point scale); Weekly Artifacts 25% (7 artifacts across T2 Weeks 1–7, assessed on 5-point rubric, weighted average); Prototype Functionality 10%; Code Quality 10%; Integration English + Math 10%; Presentation 20%; Written Design Brief 10%; Peer Review 5%.
7. Apply the minimum pass requirements from DDD Part 9.2: (1) Overall weighted score ≥55%. (2) Demo Day rubric score ≥2/4 on all 5 criteria — no zero criteria allowed. (3) At least 5 of 7 Term Two weekly artifacts submitted. (4) Minimum 6 of 8 Term Two sessions attended.
8. Apply the grading scale from DDD Part 9.1: Distinction ≥85%; Merit ≥70%; Pass ≥55%; Near Pass 45–54% (resubmission offered within 2 weeks); Not Yet <45% (Participation Acknowledgement issued, repeat stage at 50% fee reduction, parent/guardian meeting required).
9. For you who completed both Term One and Term Two: issue the Programme Completion Record listing both terms, both grades, and a portfolio description. This record is the basis for Stage 6 entry or demonstration of competency.

### CELEBRATION, CERTIFICATES AND CLOSE  (30 min)
10. Sign and prepare certificates: AI Engineer Certificate signed by Lead You and Programme Director. For Distinction achievers: note on Isoma Edutech Honour Roll, prepare written personal commendation. For Near Pass you: prepare written feedback identifying exactly which rubric criteria were not met and what resubmission evidence is required.
11. Certificate ceremony: call you's name individually. Announce their grade descriptor only — not the numerical score — to maintain dignity. "Awarded with Distinction", "Awarded with Merit", "Certificate Awarded", "Certificate pending resubmission." Whole class applauds each award. Scores are communicated privately after the ceremony.
12. Programme Completion Records issued to all you who completed both Term One and Term Two — regardless of grade. This record acknowledges the full 16-week journey.
13. You closing address (prepared in advance from 8-week participation log): name a specific achievement for you that is visible and genuine. "Over these 8 weeks, I have watched you move from blinking one LED to building a dual-sensor classifier that categorises the environment. That is real engineering growth." Address the group as a whole: specific skills they now have that they did not have in Week 1 of Term One.
14. Group photograph (parental consent verified). You may hold prototypes and certificates.
15. Parent/guardian feedback forms if attending — 4-question form: programme quality, you communication, learner growth observed at home, likelihood to recommend or continue to Stage 6.
16. Collect all peer feedback rubrics, panel score sheets, weighted score calculation worksheets, and Test Results logs. File with Assessment Records. All records retained for programme moderation and auditing purposes.
17. Inform you of Stage 6 pathway: Stage 6 builds on the AI Engineer Certificate. Key concepts in Stage 6 include: more complex sensor networks, introduction to machine learning with real datasets, and a final capstone project. The AI Engineer Certificate (or demonstrated equivalent competency) is the entry requirement.

## Stay safe
Safeguarding Note: Photo and video recording requires written parental consent obtained at enrolment — confirm the consent list before the session begins and before any device is used for recording. Photos used for marketing or social media require a separate explicit consent form signed by parent/guardian. Scores and grades are communicated privately — never announced publicly or posted on any display. Any learner who becomes distressed during the session (due to performance anxiety, an unexpected result, or any other reason) should be supported quietly outside the main group by a designated adult. Certificates for Near Pass you are held until resubmission — do not issue a certificate that has not been earned.
