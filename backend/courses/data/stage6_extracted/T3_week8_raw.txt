# Week 8 - Lesson 1

## Session length
**60 minutes**

## Today you will
* Present your integrated Arduino system to the class in under 2 minutes
* Answer two audience questions using evidence from your Test Results table

## Let's begin
1. Set up your board and open Serial before your turn starts.
2. Deliver the demo script: problem → sensors → states → outputs.
3. Trigger one ALARM live (or show a recorded log if hardware fails).
4. Take two questions; point to your Test Results table for evidence.

## Check your understanding
* Did you stay under 2 minutes?
* Did at least one answer cite a real test case?

## Key words
* showcase
* evidence
* audience question

# Week 8 - Lesson 2

## Session length
**60 minutes**

## Finish the week
* Write a short reflection: what worked, what broke, what you would add next term
* Peer-review one other project using three stars and one wish

## Continue
1. Complete the reflection sheet (5–8 sentences).
2. Visit one other team's station; give three stars and one wish on their card.
3. Receive feedback on your own project and note one change you agree with.
4. Pack kits carefully; return sensors and leads to the correct bags.

## Check your understanding
* Hand in reflection + peer card to your teacher before you leave.

## Key words
* reflection
* peer review
* three stars and a wish
