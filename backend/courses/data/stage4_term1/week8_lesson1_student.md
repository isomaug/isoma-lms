# Week 8 - Lesson 1

## Session length
**About 30 minutes** (you will finish this week in Lesson 2)

## Today you will learn
- Make final improvements to the Week 7 project based on peer review feedback
- Deliver a structured 3-minute oral presentation: Problem → Solution → Demo → What I Learned → What I Would Improve
- Demonstrate the live Micro:bit programme running without help
- State and explain at least one numerical or mathematical element of the project to the audience
- Complete a peer feedback rubric for at least one other presenter
- Receive the RoboCreator Certificate upon meeting the pass threshold (≥ 55% overall weighted score)

## Big idea
A great engineer does not just build something – they explain it, show it working, reflect on what they learned, and say what they would improve. Presenting your work clearly is a skill just as important as coding it.

## What you need
- 4 BBC Micro:bit v2 kits with USB cables (projects pre-loaded from Week 7)
- Presentation structure card – 5 sections with sentence starters
- Facilitator assessment rubric (DDD Part 8.5)
- Peer feedback rubric cards
- RoboCreator Certificates
- Projected screen or TV

## Links to English and Maths
- **English:** Structured oral presentation (about 3 minutes) with all 5 sections. Sentence starters: 'The problem I noticed was…', 'My solution works by…', 'As you can see from the demo…', 'The most important thing I learned was…', 'If I had more time, I would…'.
- **Maths:** State and explain one numerical element connected to the code (for example trigger temperature, loop counts, or scene timing fractions).

## Let's begin

### FINAL FIXES – Prepare and Rehearse
1. Review your Week 7 design brief and peer feedback.
2. Make final improvements to your project based on that feedback.
3. Check the programme is downloaded and running on the Micro:bit.
4. Practise the 5-section presentation using sentence starters:
   - Problem: The problem I noticed was…
   - Solution: My solution works by…
   - Demo: As you can see from the demo…
   - Learned: The most important thing I learned was…
   - Improve: If I had more time, I would…
5. Practise your maths statement: One mathematical element in my project is…
6. Technical check: confirm your Micro:bit programme runs before presentations begin.
