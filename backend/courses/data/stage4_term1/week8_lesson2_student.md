# Week 8 - Lesson 2

## Session length
**About 30 minutes** (finish Week 8)

## Today you will finish learning
Complete the remaining activities from this week's lesson plan, then check your understanding.

## Finish the week

### PRESENTATIONS – RoboCreator Showcase
1. Present for up to 3 minutes (+ 1 minute for questions). Cover all 5 sections in order.
2. At Demo, run the programme live. Then explain one maths element with a number, calculation, or data point.
3. While others present, complete a written peer feedback rubric (at least one sentence, not only ticks).

### DEBRIEF – Reflect and Celebrate
4. Reflect as a group: What coding concept did many projects use? What maths concept appeared more than once?
5. Read your peer feedback. Write: Based on feedback, if I built Version 2, I would…
6. Celebrate the effort: reading briefs, designing before building, using sensors, and presenting.

### CERTIFICATE CEREMONY
7. Learners who engaged, completed artifacts, and presented demonstrate being a RoboCreator.
8. Receive your RoboCreator Certificate when you meet the pass threshold.
9. Look ahead: Stage 5–6 moves toward text-based programming. Skills from this term go with you – reading code, designing before building, sensors, and presenting.

## Check your understanding
- Summative project (35%): functionality, coding depth, English connection, maths connection (DDD 8.5)
- Summative oral presentation (25%): all 5 sections, vocabulary, engagement, maths figure stated
- Peer feedback rubric collected

## Key words to remember
presentation · demo · audience · certificate · evaluate · reflect · iteration · prototype · RoboCreator · showcase

## Stay safe
Low-anxiety presentations. Scores are not announced publicly. Certificates follow the pass threshold privately if needed, with a resubmission path.
