## Finish the week

### DELIVER – Graph and calculate (finish)
1. **Step 3:** Plot your 6 readings on the line graph. Connect the points. Check title, axis labels, units, and scale.
2. **Step 4:** Calculate mean (sum ÷ 6) and range (max − min). Write both under the data table.

### DISCOVER – Analyse and report
3. Does your temperature go up, go down, or stay roughly the same? What might cause change?
4. Write your Conclusion (minimum 2 sentences): We found that… The data showed that… One real-world use for this sensor would be…
5. Complete the report frame: Aim / Method / Results (graph) / Conclusion (at least 5 sentences total).
