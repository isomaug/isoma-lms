# Week 1 - Lesson 2

## Session length
**About half of this week's plan** (finish what you started in Lesson 1)

## Finish the week
- Unbox and set up a BBC Micro:bit v2 and connect it to a laptop via USB
- Identify and name at least 4 hardware features of the Micro:bit (LED matrix, buttons A & B, USB port, edge connector)
- Open Microsoft MakeCode in a browser and navigate to the coding workspace
- Programme the Micro:bit to scroll your name on the LED display using the 'on start' block
- Programme Button A to show a happy face and Button B to show a sad face
- Define the words 'input' and 'output' in their own words and give one example of each from today's session

## Continue

### DELIVER – Programme the Micro:bit (30 min)
1. Step 1 (8 min): In the 'on start' block, find 'show string' and type your first name. Download and run. Observe: does the name scroll across the LEDs?
2. Step 2 (8 min): Drag a 'on button A pressed' block. Inside, add 'show icon – happy face'. Download and run. Press Button A and check.
3. Step 3 (8 min): Drag a 'on button B pressed' block. Inside, add 'show icon – sad face'. Download and run. Press Button B and check.
4. Step 4 (6 min): Continue with the next steps yourself. Modify the Button A icon to show a different icon of their choice. Re-download and run.
### DISCOVER – Share and Reflect (8 min)
5. Hold up their Micro:bit and presses Button A and B to show the group their icons. One sentence: 'Our Micro:bit shows ___ when we press Button A.'
6. Ask yourself 3 you: 'What is the INPUT in your programme? What is the OUTPUT?'
7. Reflection: thumbs up (understood input/output), flat hand (partly), thumbs down (still confused). Note who needs support.
8. Teaser: 'Next week, we will READ existing code and PREDICT what it does – like reading a story!'
### WRAP-UP – Save and Pack Down (2 min)
9. Follow these steps: click the project name field in MakeCode and rename to 'Week1_[Name]'. You do the same.
10. Disconnect Micro:bit from USB carefully. Return to kit box. Close laptop lids.

## Check your understanding
- Formative (During)
- Observation checklist: Can learner identify 4 Micro:bit parts? (Yes / Partially / Not yet)
- Can learner explain 'input' and 'output' using the session as an example? (oral check)
- Name scrolling on LED display and button icons working? (screen observation)
- Exit Assessment
- Learner holds up 1 finger for each task completed: ① Micro:bit connected ② Name scrolls on LED ③ Button A shows icon ④ Button B shows different icon ⑤ Can define 'input'. You records count for you.
- Differentiation
- Support: Pre-open MakeCode and have 'show string' block already placed. Provide a printed word-by-word guide. Extension: Programme button A+B pressed together to show a custom icon learner draws on the grid worksheet first.
