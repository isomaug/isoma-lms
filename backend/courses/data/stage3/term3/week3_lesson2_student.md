# Week 3 - Lesson 2

## Session length
**About half of this week's plan** (finish what you started in Lesson 1)

## Finish the week
- Identify the accelerometer as a sensor that measures the strength and direction of movement, and locate it on the Micro:bit v2 hardware diagram
- Programme the Micro:bit to display a live accelerometer strength reading ('acceleration strength' block in MakeCode) every 3 seconds using a forever loop with a pause
- Collect Dataset A: 6 accelerometer strength readings while holding the Micro:bit completely still on a flat surface
- Collect Dataset B: 6 accelerometer strength readings while slowly tilting the Micro:bit to 45 degrees and back
- Collect Dataset C: 6 accelerometer strength readings while gently shaking the Micro:bit up and down
- Plot all three datasets on one comparative line graph using three different colours; label axes, title, and legend correctly
- Calculate mean and range for each dataset; write a 7-sentence comparative analysis addressing: what each dataset showed, key differences between datasets, what the data means physically, and one limitation of the experiment

## Continue

### DELIVER – Code, Collect Three Datasets, and Plot (30 min)
1. Step 1 – Build the programme (5 min): code forever -> show number acceleration strength -> pause 3000ms -> clear screen. Download and run. Confirm readings appear. Ask: 'What is the reading right now, held still?' Confirm it is approximately 1000 mg.
2. Step 2 – Collect Dataset A: Still (7 min): Micro:bit placed flat on the desk, undisturbed. Each time a reading flashes, Navigator reads it aloud, Driver records in Dataset A. 6 readings. Both you record independently.
3. Step 3 – Collect Dataset B: Tilting (7 min): Driver slowly tilts the Micro:bit to approximately 45 degrees and returns to flat, once per reading cycle. 6 readings recorded in Dataset B.
4. Step 4 – Collect Dataset C: Shaking (6 min): Continue with the next steps yourself. New Driver gently shakes the Micro:bit up and down during each reading interval — consistently but not violently. 6 readings recorded in Dataset C.
5. Step 5 – Plot the triple graph (5 min): using three coloured pencils, plot Dataset A (Colour 1), Dataset B (Colour 2), Dataset C (Colour 3) on the graph worksheet. Connect each set of points with its colour. Confirm the legend is filled in.
### DISCOVER – Calculate, Write, and Compare (8 min)
6. You calculate mean and range for all three datasets. Complete the summary statistics table at the bottom of the data worksheet. Ask yourself: 'Which dataset has the greatest range? What does that tell us about consistency?'
7. You independently writes the 7-sentence comparative analysis using the writing frame. Check your work carefully: 'Sentence 7 asks about a limitation — what is one thing about this experiment that might make the results unreliable? Think about how we collected the data.'
8. Two you share their graphs on the projected screen. Class reads data from the graph together: 'At which reading was the gap between A and C greatest? How closely did Dataset B follow Dataset C?' Compare predictions (hypotheses) to actual results.
### WRAP-UP – Save and Collect (2 min)
9. Rename MakeCode project 'T3W3_[Name]_Accel'. Disconnect Micro:bit.
10. Collect triple data tables, triple line graphs, and comparative analysis frames — all three are formally assessed artifacts this week. You checks: all 18 data entries filled, three coloured lines on graph with legend, 7 sentences written with correct vocabulary.

## Check your understanding
- Formative (During)
- Observation: Does the Micro:bit display live accelerometer strength readings correctly every 3 seconds? (functionality check)
- Are all three data tables complete with 6 readings each, correct headings, and notes? (data collection check)
- Triple line graph plotted with three coloured lines, title, labelled axes, and completed legend? (graph check)
- Mean and range calculated correctly for all three datasets and recorded in summary statistics table? (maths check)
- Exit Assessment
- You holds up one pair's triple graph and asks you: ① 'Which condition produced the highest mean — A, B, or C?' ② 'What was the range for Dataset C?' ③ 'Read me Sentence 6 from your analysis — what does the accelerometer measure?' 3 points for you. You records on assessment log alongside the graph and analysis artifact grade.
- Differentiation
- Support: Reduce to two datasets (A and C — Still and Shaking; skip Tilting). Plot both on the same graph using two coloured lines. Reduce the analysis to 5 sentences. Allow learner to use a calculator for mean calculation. Extension: Collect a fourth dataset (Dataset D — walking slowly while holding the Micro:bit) and add a fourth line to the graph in a fourth colour. Calculate the median of each dataset in addition to the mean (requires sorting the 6 readings in order first). Write a paragraph comparing mean vs. median: which gives a better picture of typical movement for each condition and why?
