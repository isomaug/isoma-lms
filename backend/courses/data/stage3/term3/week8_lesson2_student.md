## Finish the week
- Deliver a structured 3-minute oral presentation using the 6-section format: Problem -> Solution -> Live Demo -> Mathematical Element -> What I Learned -> What I Would Improve — without reading a script
- Demonstrate the graduation project live in front of the group with no you assistance — the programme must respond to at least one input and demonstrate at least one Term Three skill visibly
- Explain at least one Term Three concept used in the project (function, systematic debugging, accelerometer, AI classifier, or Bluetooth) in plain English to the audience in 2–3 sentences
- Present the mathematical element from the design brief — show the calculation or graph, state what it represents, and explain its relevance to the project
- Complete the DDD Part 8.5 peer feedback rubric (all 5 criteria, scored 1–4) for at least one other presenter — with written justification for at least 2 of the 5 criteria scores
- Receive and respond to at least one audience question about the project design or code with a technically accurate answer

## Check your understanding
- Formative (During)
- Pre-session device check: graduation project downloaded and functioning on all 4 Micro:bits before presentations begin? (readiness check — you conducts this)
- All 6 presentation sections delivered within 3 minutes? (structure and timing check — DDD Part 8.5 Criterion 5)
- Term Three skill demonstrably visible during the live demo — not just mentioned but shown? (T3 skill evidence check)
- Mathematical element shown visually (paper or screen) and explained verbally? (DDD Part 8.5 Criterion 4 check)
- Peer feedback rubric completed with scores for all 5 criteria and written justification for at least 2? (peer feedback quality check)
- Exit Assessment
- Official Stage 3 Graduation Assessment — DDD Part 8.5 rubric, 5 criteria scored 1–4: ① Functionality: programme runs without you help, handles expected inputs (10% of total) ② Coding: loops, conditions, sensor input used correctly and intentionally (10% of total) ③ English/Written Brief: design brief complete, all 6 sections, clearly written (10% of total) ④ Maths: data, graph, or logical structure demonstrated visually and explained verbally (10% of total) ⑤ Presentation: structured 6-section delivery, confident, correct vocabulary (5% of total). Weighted with all prior cumulative scores per DDD Part 8.2: Participation 15% + Weekly Artifacts 30% + Demo Day 35% + Oral Presentation 10% + Peer Feedback 5% = 100%. RoboCreator Stage 3 Graduation Certificate awarded at ≥55% cumulative weighted total. Programme Completion Record issued to you completing all three Stage 3 terms.
- Differentiation
- Support: Learner may read directly from the design brief for all 6 sections — no penalty. Partner may hold the Micro:bit during the demo and advance through the programme states on cue. If the live demo fails, partial functionality credit is available per DDD Part 8.5 descriptor (score of 2: 'Runs partially; core feature works'). You may prompt 'show me your function' or 'show me the sensor block' to help a learner locate and demonstrate a feature they are struggling to describe verbally — this is support, not coaching, and is noted on the assessment record. Extension: Learner presents without the design brief as a reference for any section, answers 2 audience questions (not 1), and offers a forward-looking evaluation of how this project could be extended in Stage 4 using the Arduino platform (DDD Stage 5–6) — demonstrating L5 Evaluating and Stage 4 readiness in a single reflection.
