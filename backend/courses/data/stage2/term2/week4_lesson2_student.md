# Week 4 - Lesson 2

## Session length
**About half of this week's plan** (finish what you started in Lesson 1)

## Finish the week
- Write an independent project brief including a decomposition of at least 4 sub-problems with individual test conditions
- Build an interactive 2-page programme using at least 5 skill types from Term 1 and Term 2 combined
- Independently debug at least one issue using the 3-step process without you assistance
- Present the completed programme in 2 minutes, explaining the algorithm, the decomposition approach, and the hardest bug fixed

## Continue

### PRESENT AND EVALUATE (13 min)
1. You checkpoint at 15 minutes: "How many sub-problems tested and ticked?" Any pair behind receives a 2-minute you support session — after which they are on your own.
2. At 25 minutes: review progress. If 4 sub-problems complete — begin polishing (add backgrounds, sounds, tighten timing). If fewer than 3 complete — continue building only the most critical sub-problems.
3. Bug encountered: apply 3-step debug independently. If the bug takes more than 5 minutes: document it in the evaluation section ("Known issue: ___. Cause: ___. Attempted fix: ___. Result: still present / resolved.") and move on.
4. BUILD TIMER stops at 35 minutes. Save project immediately.
5. Get ready to use presentation structure cards. 2 minutes of silent preparation (you re-read their structure card and the brief).
6. Presentation round: you present to adjacent you. Strictly 2 minutes each. Audience completes peer evaluation form.

### WRAP-UP (0 min — included in phase above)
7. Self-evaluation: score 3 criteria (0/1/2 per criterion). Calculate total and percentage.
8. Read peer evaluation form received. Identify: did the audience correctly identify the most important skill? Did they pick up one vocabulary word?
9. Ask yourself one learner for you: "What was the hardest sub-problem — and what did you learn from it?"
10. Complete Term 2 Skills Passport stamps.
11. Collect all briefs, evaluation forms, and peer forms for portfolios.
12. Preview: "Weeks 5–8 of Term 2 — we build a complete interactive game that uses everything from BOTH terms. Prepare to be challenged!"

## Check your understanding
- Formative (During)
- Independent brief and decomposition approved: 4 sub-problems with test conditions, 5+ skills ticked
- You checkpoint at 15 min: sub-problem progress noted for you
- At least 1 independent debug documented in evaluation section
- Self-evaluation score completed and compared to peer evaluation
- Term 2 Skills Passport: minimum 5 of 6 stamps for Term 2 readiness
- Exit / Summative
- You reviews self-evaluation grids. Ask each pair one follow-up question based on their self-score. You who scored themselves 6/6 but have an incomplete programme: "What criterion do you think was not fully met? What is the evidence?" Target honest self-reflection over optimistic scoring.
- Differentiation
- Support: Reduce to 2 sub-problems, 3 skills, 2 success criteria. You assists with brief writing. Extension: Write a short "programmer's commentary" for the project — 5–8 sentences describing: the concept, the algorithm design, one technical challenge, the solution, and what you would build next if this were a real app.
