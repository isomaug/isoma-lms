# Week 4 - Lesson 1

## Session length
**About half of this week's plan** (you will finish in Lesson 2)

## Today you will learn
- Write an independent project brief including a decomposition of at least 4 sub-problems with individual test conditions
- Build an interactive 2-page programme using at least 5 skill types from Term 1 and Term 2 combined
- Independently debug at least one issue using the 3-step process without you assistance
- Present the completed programme in 2 minutes, explaining the algorithm, the decomposition approach, and the hardest bug fixed

## Big idea
The highest skill in programming is not knowing the most blocks — it is designing a programme that works, planning it carefully, and evaluating it honestly. Today you do all three independently.

## What you need
- 4 tablets with Stack KIDI — clean new projects

## Links to English and Maths
- **English:** The 2-minute presentation requires: clear spoken English, subject vocabulary, logical structure, and the ability to explain a complex process simply. You rehearse once before presenting. Presentation evaluation language: "The algorithm works by ___. The hardest bug was ___ because ___ . I fixed it by ___." Vocabulary: interactive, performance, present, algorithm, justify, reflect.
- **Maths:** Skills count: you count how many of the 10 listed skills appear in their programme. Calculate percentage: skills used ÷ 10 × 100 = ___%. Self-evaluation score: 3 criteria × 2 marks each = 6 maximum. Convert to percentage. Compare self-score percentage to skills percentage — do they correlate?

## Let's begin

### DESIGN SPRINT — Independent Brief and Decomposition (12 min)
1. Independent Project Brief and Decomposition Sheet (A4 for you, combined): Title | Story/Interaction concept | Decomposition (4 sub-problems with test conditions) | Skills used checklist (10 skills from Terms 1+2) | 3 self-written success criteria | Self-evaluation section
2. Project timer card (projected): DESIGN = 12 min / BUILD = 35 min / PRESENT + EVALUATE = 13 min
3. Presentation structure card (A5): 2-minute structure: ① Title and concept (20s) ② Run the programme live (45s) ③ Explain your algorithm and one skill used (25s) ④ Describe the hardest bug you fixed (20s) ⑤ Self-evaluation score out of 6 (10s)
4. Peer evaluation form (A5): ① The skill most clearly demonstrated: ___ ② One vocabulary word heard: ___ ③ One suggestion: ___
5. Term 2 Skills Passport (A5): stamp sheet for Variables, Wait, Decomposition, Complex Algorithm, Independent Brief, Presentation
6. Get ready to use combined brief and decomposition sheets. Timer starts: 12 minutes.

### BUILD SPRINT — Code the Interactive Performance (35 min)
7. Write: title, 1-sentence concept, and the audience this performance is designed for.
8. Decompose into 4 sub-problems. For each: name the sub-problem, list the blocks, write the test condition.
9. Tick the skills checklist — target minimum 5 of 10 skills.
10. Write 3 success criteria (specific and testable — same rules as Lesson 12 Stage 1).
11. You approval at the 12-minute mark. Any pair without an approved brief must simplify to 2 sub-problems before coding starts — no exceptions.
12. Build sub-problem by sub-problem. Test each before connecting (as practised in Lesson 11).
