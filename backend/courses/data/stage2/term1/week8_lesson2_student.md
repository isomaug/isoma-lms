# Week 8 - Lesson 2

## Session length
**About half of this week's plan** (finish what you started in Lesson 1)

## Finish the week
- Deliver a 90-second structured presentation to a guest audience, explaining algorithm, loop, and message used
- Demonstrate the project running live and narrate each character's programme as it runs
- Use at least 5 computing vocabulary terms correctly during the presentation
- Complete a peer evaluation form for one other pair's project, providing evidence-based feedback

## Continue

### DISCOVER — Reflection, Awards, Passport Stamping (13 min)
1. You moves between stations completing the rubric for each pair while listening.
2. When not presenting, you complete a Peer Evaluation Form for one other pair they visited.
3. Final 5 minutes of rotation: you can ask guests one question — "What was the most surprising thing you learned about coding today?" Record the answer.
4. Guests seated. Read out top scores and quotes best vocabulary usage heard during presentations.
5. Computing Passport: you reads each Term 1 skill. You hold up the passport — you stamps/ticks skills demonstrated today.
6. Reflection: "Term 1 started with learning what an algorithm is. Today you explained algorithms to adults. What is the biggest change in how you think about computers?" Open discussion.

### WRAP-UP (4 min)
7. Certificate presentation: you is called by name. Certificate awarded with one personal acknowledgement from the you.
8. Guest question answered as a class: "What surprised them most?" Discuss.
9. Collect peer evaluation forms, rubrics, and project briefs for portfolios.
10. Save projects — label: "Stage 2 Term 1 Final Project — [Pair Names]."
11. Collect tablets. Return to charging station.
12. You note: review rubric scores and Computing Passports tonight. Identify any learner requiring additional support or extension in Term 2.

## Check your understanding
- Formative (During)
- Demo Day rubric scored by you: 5 criteria × 3 = 15 maximum. Score ≥10 = ready for Stage 2 Term 2
- At least 5 computing vocabulary terms used correctly (tally on rubric)
- Loop AND message both demonstrated live in front of guest audience
- Peer Evaluation Form completed with evidence-based feedback
- Computing Passport: minimum 5 of 6 Term 1 skills ticked
- Exit / Summative
- Computing Passport completion. Write down: total skills ticked for you. Any learner below 4/6 receives a targeted support plan for Term 2. Any learner at 6/6 receives an "Enrichment Challenge" card for home.
- Differentiation
- Support: Learner is paired with a stronger presenter who leads the guest interaction; learner demonstrates the Green Flag press and names the characters. Extension: Learner prepares and delivers a 30-second "What is an algorithm?" explanation as an opening address to all guests before station rotations begin.
