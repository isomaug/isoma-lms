# Week 7 - Lesson 1

## Session length
**About half of this week's plan** (you will finish in Lesson 2)

## Today you will learn
- Plan a complete 2-page, 2-character interactive story using a project brief template before writing any code
- Apply at least four different types of block from Term 1: movement, sound, loop, message, trigger
- Use the 3-step debugging process independently to identify and fix at least one bug encountered during the build
- Evaluate the completed project against a 4-criterion success checklist and give it a self-assessed score

## Big idea
A real project uses ALL the skills together. Planning before coding always produces a better result than coding without a plan - even when you only have 60 minutes.

## What you need
- 4 tablets with Stack KIDI (clean new projects for each pair)

## Links to English and Maths
- **English:** Project brief as a formal planning document - you experience the discipline of planning completely before acting. Narrative writing: story must have a clear beginning (Page 1) and resolution (Page 2). Presentation vocabulary: "My project uses ___ because ___." Evaluation: "I am most proud of ___ in my project. If I had more time I would ___."
- **Maths:** Project management: time allocation - 15 min design, 35 min build, 10 min evaluate. You self-manage against a visible countdown timer. Success criteria scoring: add up self-assessment scores (max 8). Calculate percentage: score ÷ 8 × 100 = ___%. Compare to previous lesson scores.

## Let's begin

### DESIGN SPRINT - Project Brief (15 min)
1. Project Brief Template (A4 for you) - sections: Story Title | Characters (2) | Page 1 plan | Page 2 plan | Block types to use (checklist) | Success Criteria (4 items)
2. Project planning storyboard (A3, 4 frames) - 2 frames for Page 1, 2 frames for Page 2
3. Block type checklist sticker sheet - small stickers: movement, sound, loop, message, trigger, background. You stick them onto the brief as they use each type
4. Success Criteria checklist (A5 for you): ① 2 pages with different backgrounds ② 2 characters with different programmes ③ At least 1 loop used ④ Characters communicate using a message
5. Self-assessment scoring card: 0 = not yet | 1 = partially | 2 = fully achieved, for each of 4 criteria (max 8 points)
6. Get ready to use Project Brief Templates and storyboards. Timer starts: 15 minutes to complete all planning.

### DELIVER SPRINT - Build (35 min)
7. Step 1 (4 min): Story title + 2 characters named, coloured, and described in one sentence each.
8. Step 2 (5 min): Page 1 plan - background, what Character 1 does, what Character 2 does, what message is sent at the end.
9. Step 3 (4 min): Page 2 plan - new background, what happens after the message arrives.
10. Step 4 (2 min): Tick the block type checklist - which 4+ types will be used? You reviews each brief and approves. No pair moves to a device until the brief is approved.
11. Reminder: "The brief is your ALGORITHM. If you change something while coding, update the brief too."
12. Timer: 35 minutes. You open new Stack KIDI projects.
