## Check your understanding
- Formative (During)
- Team Polish Checklist: minimum 6/7 items ticked for Demo Day clearance
- Presentation Role Assignment Card: all 4 roles assigned, all 4 you have bullet points
- Rehearsal Round 2: improvement from Round 1 observed (you Feedback Card)
- Final Evaluation Grid: consensus score recorded, total and percentage calculated
- Exit / Summative
- You runs the programme on the integration tablet once more - does it pass item 7 of the Polish Checklist (runs without help)? If YES: Demo Day ready. If NO: you identifies the single highest-priority remaining issue and fixes it with the group after the session.
- Differentiation
- Support: Simplify the speaking role to one sentence only ("My name is ___ and I built the ___"). You scaffolds the sentence by asking questions the learner answers aloud. Extension: Prepare an additional "technical deep dive" card that can be offered to any guest who asks a follow-up question - covering one specific computing concept in detail (e.g. "Here is how our interface message works").

## Stay safe
Safeguarding Note: Presenting in a group reduces individual anxiety but can create pressure for quieter you. Remind all groups: "Every speaking role is equally important. The person who explains the integration is not more important than the person who introduces the title." Give shy you the Introducer role - it is the shortest and most clearly scripted.
