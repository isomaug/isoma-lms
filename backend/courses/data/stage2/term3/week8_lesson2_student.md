## Check your understanding
- Formative (During)
- Final Showcase Rubric: 7 criteria × 3 = 21 max. Score for you recorded by you + assessor
- AI adaptive response explained to at least one guest using correct vocabulary
- Community Guest Play narrated in real time (all 4 members contributed)
- Stage 2 Learning Contract read back and progress against goals stated publicly
- Exit / Summative
- Stage 2 Year Completion Assessment: you completes a final individual record for you:
- ① Strongest computational thinking skill demonstrated
- ② Stage 3 readiness: Distinction (≥85% mean rubric score across all three terms) / Merit (≥70%) / Pass (≥55%)
- ③ One personal note for the Stage 3 you handover. This document is the most important educational record produced this year.
- Differentiation
- Support: Learner presents only the Title and Story Summary section of the team presentation (20 seconds). Other team members cover the rest. Learner still participates in Q&A with one prepared answer. Extension: Learner writes and delivers a 90-second closing address to all guests at the end of the ceremony - summarising what the whole class achieved this year and why it matters for Uganda's future. This is an advanced public speaking task that requires synthesis of the entire year.

## Stay safe
Safeguarding Note: As for all large public events: all guests registered, photography consent obtained, you present throughout, no learner alone with an adult at any time. The Year Reflection Scrolls contain personal reflective writing - do not display or read these without explicit learner consent. Certificate documents should note that they are educational records, not legal qualifications. Store all assessment records securely and in line with the school's data protection policy.
