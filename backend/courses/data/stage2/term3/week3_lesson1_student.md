# Week 3 - Lesson 1

## Session length
**About half of this week's plan** (you will finish in Lesson 2)

## Today you will learn
- Apply a 7-item team polish checklist to the integrated project, achieving a minimum of 6/7 items ticked
- Assign a specific speaking role to each of the four team members for the Demo Day presentation
- Rehearse a 2-minute team presentation where each member speaks for approximately 30 seconds about their part
- Score the integrated project against all 4 integrated success criteria and produce a final self-evaluation score

## Big idea
Polishing a team project is different from polishing a solo project. Every decision must be agreed. Every change must be tested. The most powerful polish is the one that the whole team agrees makes the project better.

## What you need
- 2 tablets per group of 4 - integrated project files from Lesson 18
- Team Polish Checklist (A5 per group): 7 items –
- ☐ Both sub-systems run correctly
- ☐ Interface triggers cleanly
- ☐ No sound clashes between sub-systems
- ☐ Backgrounds visually distinct between pages
- ☐ Title screen present
- ☐ End sequence present
- ☐ Full programme runs without you intervention

## Links to English and Maths
- **English:** Team presentation structure: each of the 4 team members has a specific and non-overlapping speaking role. The group must ensure that all four parts connect smoothly - no repetition, no gaps. You practise the handover: "I will now pass over to ___ who will explain ___." Vocabulary: handover, transition, integration, demonstrate, explain, evaluate, contribute, collaborate.
- **Maths:** Final evaluation: 4 criteria × 2 points each = 8 maximum. Sum scores. Convert to percentage. Compare this term's collaborative project score to the individual project scores from Term 2. Is the collaborative score higher or lower? What factors explain the difference?

## Let's begin

### HOOK - The Premiere (6 min)
1. Presentation Role Assignment Card (A5 per group): 4 roles - Introducer (title + concept) / Sub-system A Explainer / Sub-system B Explainer / Integration Explainer + Self-Evaluator. You writes their name next to their role
2. Team Rehearsal Feedback Card (A5, used by you): "Person 1 - clear? YES/PARTLY/NO. Vocabulary word used? ___. One tip: ___." One row per team member
3. Final Evaluation Grid (A4 per group): 4 integrated success criteria × 3-point scale (0/1/2) + group-level reflection section
4. You: "Tonight is the premiere. Tomorrow the public sees your project. Today is the last chance to make it perfect."
5. Each group runs their integrated project from start to finish - silently. You watches. Notes anything that needs fixing on the Team Polish Checklist.
6. Groups receive the blank checklist. Together, they run the project again and tick each item themselves. Any unticked item = a fix that must happen in the build phase.

### DELIVER - Team Polish Sprint (25 min)
7. Rule: "Every fix today must be agreed by the whole group before it is made. No individual changes without discussion."
8. Groups work on their one tablet. Build polish items systematically from the checklist. Each fix agreed verbally before implementing.
9. Item 1 - Sub-system coherence (4 min): run each sub-system independently. Any issue remaining from Lesson 18 - fix now. Tick.
10. Item 2 - Interface clean run (3 min): run the full programme. Interface fires correctly? Tick.
11. Item 3 - Sound design pass (4 min): listen to all sounds in sequence. Any clash? Adjust volume positions. Tick.
