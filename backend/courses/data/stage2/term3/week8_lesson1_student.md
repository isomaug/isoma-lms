# Week 8 - Lesson 1

## Session length
**About half of this week's plan** (you will finish in Lesson 2)

## Today you will learn
- Deliver the full 2-minute team presentation to multiple rounds of community guests, maintaining quality and confidence across all rounds
- Handle a panel Q&A from community guests - including at least one question about the AI adaptive response - using subject vocabulary and programme evidence
- Facilitate a community guest playing the collaborative programme and narrate their experience in real time
- Complete the Stage 2 Year Completion Assessment and receive the Stage 2 Certificate

## Big idea
Every line of code you wrote this year started as a thought in your head. You turned a thought into a plan, a plan into a programme, a programme into a story or a game, and today - a story or game into a public achievement. That journey from thought to audience is what every software engineer, game designer, and digital artist does for their career. You have done it. In Year 2. Completely.

## What you need
- 2 tablets per group - AI-Enhanced Final Project (locked from Lesson 21)
- Technical Portfolio Folders (for you, sealed from Lesson 22)
- Personalised Showcase Briefing Cards from Lesson 23 (you prepared)

## Links to English and Maths
- **English:** The highest-level English performance task of the stage: a team public presentation with panel Q&A that demands technical vocabulary, logical explanation, narrative coherence, and interpersonal confidence - simultaneously. The community audience includes non-technical adults who require analogies and accessible language. The skill of "explaining computing to a non-specialist" is one of the most in-demand professional skills in Uganda's growing tech sector. Vocabulary: algorithm, state, decompose, integrate, adaptive, iterate, playtest, evidence, achievement, legacy.
- **Maths:** Class-level statistics for the Community Impact Statement: you calculates total lessons attended, total programmes built, total bugs debugged (estimated from Bug Reports and Debug Logs across all three terms), total playtests conducted, and total Demo Days. These are presented as a data-driven narrative of the year. Individual you calculate their personal rubric average across all three terms.

## Let's begin

### SETUP - The Professional Exhibition (15 min)
1. Final Showcase Rubric (A4 for you, scored jointly by you and an invited technical assessor): 7 criteria - Programme Runs Completely / All 4 Members Speak / Algorithm Explained / Collaborative Design Described / AI Feature Explained / Q&A Handling / Confidence and Clarity - each 0–3 (max 21)
2. Community Guest Observation Forms (A5, 2 per guest): "The team that impressed me most was ___ because ___. One computing concept I now understand: ___. My question for the team: ___."
3. Stage 2 Year Completion Certificate (A5, pre-printed for you): "This certifies that ___ has completed Stage 2 of the Isoma Edutech Design · Deliver · Discover Programme, demonstrating advanced computational thinking, collaborative programme design, software testing, and AI awareness across three full terms."
4. Year Reflection Scroll (A4, rolled and tied with a ribbon for you): learner's own Year-Long Progress Narrative from Lesson 22, printed and prepared as a keepsake
5. Community Impact Statement (A3 poster, displayed): prepared by you - "This class built ___ programmes, attended ___ lessons, fixed ___ bugs, playtested ___ times, and presented at ___ Demo Days. They are ready for Stage 3."
6. Room set up as a professional technology exhibition: 2 team stations with tablets, portfolio folders open, programme screenshots displayed, Community Impact Statement poster visible.
7. You arrive to find Personalised Showcase Briefing Cards at their stations. You read your card silently.

### DELIVER - The Final Showcase (45 min)
8. Final device checks: both tablets running. AI adaptive response tested. You confirms: "Both branches fire correctly? Title screen loads? Win state triggers?" Only YES answers proceed.
9. You walks the line: one personal encouragement for you. Specific, true, personal.
10. Rule reminder: "If a question is about your partner pair's sub-system - you still answer it. You are ONE team. You are responsible for knowing the whole programme."
11. Guests welcomed. You address: "Stage 2 you have spent this year building programmes that use algorithms, loops, variables, messages, and AI-style adaptive responses. They built alone and together. They tested with real users, iterated, and polished. Each team today will present their work and answer your questions. They have earned this."
12. Rotation 1 (10 min): guests divided between both team stations. Teams deliver 2-minute presentation. Invited technical assessor at one station, you at the other.
13. Rotation 2 (10 min): guests swap stations.
