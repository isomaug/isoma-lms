# Week 6 - Lesson 2

## Session length
**About half of this week's plan** (finish what you started in Lesson 1)

## Finish the week
- Curate a Technical Portfolio selecting one piece of evidence per term (3 total) with a written justification for each choice
- Write a technical annotation for each evidence piece explaining: what it shows, what computing concept it demonstrates, and how it compares to earlier work
- Complete a Year-Long Progress Narrative: a 5-paragraph structured reflection connecting Term 1 beginnings to Term 3 achievements
- Identify one specific skill that will be built upon in Stage 2 and write a Stage 2 Learning Contract with two measurable goals

## Continue

### DELIVER — Write the Technical Annotations and Narrative (30 min)
1. Write the 2-sentence justification for each choice. Read each justification aloud to yourself. Does it explain the "why"? If not, rewrite.
2. You reviews: "Is each selection justified? Is there clear evidence of progression from Term 1 to Term 3?"
3. ANNOTATIONS (15 min): For each of the 3 selected pieces, complete a Technical Annotation Template. The "compared to earlier work" sentence is the most important — this is where progress is made visible.
4. YEAR-LONG PROGRESS NARRATIVE (12 min): Write all 5 paragraphs using the template. Target length: 2–3 sentences per paragraph (10–15 sentences total). You who struggle with writing may dictate to the you for scribing.

### DISCOVER — Portfolio Pair Share (8 min)
5. STAGE 2 LEARNING CONTRACT (3 min): Write both goals. Goals must be specific: "I will be able to build a programme with a variable that changes over time" — not "I will get better at coding."
6. Pair up (any pairing — not the usual coding partners). Share one Technical Annotation from any term.
7. Partner reads it and responds: "The most interesting thing you wrote is ___. One thing I would like to know more about: ___."
8. Switch. Other partner shares.

### WRAP-UP (4 min)
9. Class: you ask for 3 volunteers to share their "what I proved I could do in Term 3" paragraph sentence. Celebrate each one.
10. File all completed documents into the Technical Portfolio Folder. Folder sealed.
11. You signs the Stage 2 Learning Contract. You countersigns.
12. Preview: "Next lesson — full dress rehearsal for the End-of-Year Showcase. Your portfolio will be displayed alongside your programme. Guests will read both."

## Check your understanding
- Formative (During)
- Portfolio Evidence Selection Guide: 3 selections with justified choices showing progression from Term 1 to 3
- Technical Annotations: 3 completed — "compared to earlier work" sentence present in each
- Year-Long Progress Narrative: all 5 paragraphs completed in formal register
- Stage 2 Learning Contract: 2 specific, measurable goals — you countersigned
- Exit / Summative
- Read the Paragraph 5 ("When I begin Stage 2, I am ready to ___ because ___") of every narrative. This is the clearest indicator of readiness for Stage 2. Any learner who writes "I am not ready" — you addresses directly and privately. Reframe: "You ARE ready. Here is what tells me that."
- Differentiation
- Support: Reduce the narrative to 3 paragraphs (Terms 1, 2, 3 — one each, no opening or closing). You scripts the annotation structure and learner fills in the content. Extension: Write a 1-page "Executive Summary" for the portfolio — a document written for a school governor or parent who has never attended a computing lesson. What should they understand about what was learned and why it matters?
