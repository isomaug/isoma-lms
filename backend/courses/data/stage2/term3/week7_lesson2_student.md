# Week 7 - Lesson 2

## Session length
**About half of this week's plan** (finish what you started in Lesson 1)

## Finish the week
- Deliver the complete 2-minute team presentation with each of the four members speaking their assigned section without overlap or gaps
- Handle a panel Q&A where any team member can be asked any question about any part of the programme
- Evaluate the team presentation against a 6-criteria rubric and agree on a consensus team score
- Identify one specific improvement for each team member to implement before the Final Showcase

## Continue

### DISCOVER — Team Consensus Evaluation and Improvement (20 min)
1. Rotation: audience moves. Both groups present again to a different audience subset.
2. Throughout: you notes one specific improvement action per team member on a private note.
3. Invited audience leaves. Groups collect Observation Sheets.
4. Individual scoring (4 min): each team member scores the presentation independently on all 6 criteria.
5. Consensus discussion (5 min): share scores. Where do team members agree? Where do they disagree? Negotiate a consensus score for each criterion.
6. Calculate team average. Identify the lowest-scoring criterion. This is the team's single improvement priority.
7. Individual Improvement Notes (4 min): each team member writes their personal improvement for the Final Showcase.

### WRAP-UP (9 min)
8. You shares private notes (one per team member). You read theirs, signs it, and attaches it to their portfolio.
9. One improvement rehearsal (7 min): each group rehearses the specific handover or section identified as weakest. You confirms improvement observed.
10. Save final versions of both AI-enhanced tablets. Confirm both run from start to finish independently.
11. Collect all Observation Sheets, Consensus Rubrics, and Individual Improvement Notes for portfolios.
12. You to each group: one specific personal encouragement per team member.
13. Preview: "Next lesson is the End-of-Year Final Showcase. Your Technical Portfolios will be displayed. Your programme will run in front of the whole community. Every person on this team has something to be proud of."

## Check your understanding
- Formative (During)
- All 4 team members spoke during the rehearsal (you observation)
- Panel Q&A: cross-team questions handled (at least one question answered about the other pair's sub-system)
- Team Consensus Rubric: individual and consensus scores recorded
- Individual Improvement Notes: specific, personal, and signed
- Exit / Summative
- You's Dress Rehearsal Log: one row per team member — overall readiness rating (Ready / Nearly Ready / Needs Support). Any learner rated "Needs Support" receives a private pre-Final Showcase preparation conversation, not in front of the group.
- Differentiation
- Support: Assign the learner the Introducer role — the shortest, most scripted section. You provides a complete written script for that section. Learner practises reading it naturally (not robotically). Extension: Learner prepares a supplementary "Deep Technical Explanation" card for the most complex feature (the AI adaptive response). This is offered only to audience members who ask follow-up questions about the AI element.
