# Week 4 - Lesson 2

## Session length
**About half of this week's plan** (finish what you started in Lesson 1)

## Finish the week
- Independently demonstrate 6 computing concepts by showing and explaining them in a programme (from any term) without assistance
- Make a live programme modification under timed conditions: a specific change announced by the you 2 minutes before the assessment begins
- Select and present a "best work" portfolio piece from any term and explain why it represents their strongest computational thinking
- Complete a year-long self-assessment locating personal progress across all 20+ skills taught from Term 1 Week 1 to Term 3 Week 4

## Continue

### DISCOVER — Whole-Class Reflection (10 min)
1. LIVE MODIFICATION (3 min): learner makes the modification live on the tablet. You observes — does not help.
2. CONCEPT DEMONSTRATION (4 min): You selects 4 of the 6 concepts from the Mastery Assessment Card to ask about today. For each: "Show me an example in a programme. Explain it in one sentence." Learner demonstrates and explains. You scores each.
3. PORTFOLIO PIECE (1 min): learner shares their Portfolio Selection Card and runs the selected project. 30 seconds max.
4. While 1-to-1 assessments continue, other you complete the Year-Long Skills Self-Assessment independently.
5. After all assessments: class sits together. Ask yourself three questions — hands up for responses:
6. "Which skill from Term 1 do you STILL use almost every lesson?" (e.g. algorithm, sequence)

### WRAP-UP (5 min)
7. "Which skill from Term 3 still feels most challenging?"
8. "If you could teach ONE thing you learned this year to a learner who is just starting — what would it be? Why?"
9. You read their Portfolio Selection Card reason aloud in one sentence.
10. Year-Long Skills Self-Assessment collected. Percentage calculated and recorded on the front cover of the Portfolio Folder.
11. You announces: "Lessons 21–23 are the final polish weeks. Lesson 24 is the End-of-Year Showcase — the largest Demo Day of the programme."
12. You writes one sentence about what they want to achieve in the End-of-Year Showcase: "In the final showcase, I want the audience to understand ___."

## Check your understanding
- Formative (During)
- Mastery Assessment Card: you scores for 4 of 6 concepts demonstrated live (target: minimum 5/8 = 62%)
- Live Modification: completed independently within time limit (YES/NO)
- Portfolio Selection Card: specific justification for choice (not "because it was fun")
- Year-Long Skills Self-Assessment: completed for all 22 skills — scores reviewed by you
- Exit / Summative
- You compares learner self-assessment percentage to own Mastery Assessment Card scores. If self-assessment is significantly higher than you score — learner is over-estimating. If significantly lower — learner is under-estimating. Both are discussed briefly and honestly with you in the 1-to-1. Target: realistic self-assessment that matches evidence.
- Differentiation
- Support: Reduce Mastery Assessment to 3 concepts (algorithm, loop, debug). Simplify the Live Modification to a pre-agreed change that the learner has practised. Extension: Learner produces a 1-minute "Computing Concept Video" by recording their screen and voice (if device supports it) explaining their best project. This becomes a showcase artefact for parents.
